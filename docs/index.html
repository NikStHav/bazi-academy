FAQ

Why is my year pillar different from my Chinese zodiac sign?
BaZi starts the year at Li Chun (around 4 February), not at Chinese New Year. If you were born in January or early February, your BaZi year is the previous one.

Which time zone should I enter?
The offset that applied at your birthplace on that date, including daylight saving time.
