BaZi Academy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

BaZi Academy (“the app”, Android package com.baziacademy.app) is an independent learning app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The birth dates, times, names and settings you enter to calculate charts, and your lesson progress and quiz scores, are stored only locally on your device (in the app’s private storage). They never leave your device. The app requests no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

You can delete individual charts in the app, reset your progress on the “About” screen, or remove all data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

No tracking, advertising or analytics

The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no user accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
