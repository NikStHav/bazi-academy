BaZi Academy – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Deutsche Fassung · Versión en español

BaZi Academy (“the app”, app ID app.baziacademy on Android and iOS) is published by NSH Holding GmbH (see legal notice). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The birth dates, times, birthplaces, names and settings you enter to calculate charts, and your lesson progress and quiz scores, are stored only locally on your device (in the app’s private storage). They never leave your device. The app works offline and contains no code that sends your data anywhere (on Android it does not even request the internet permission).

You can delete individual charts in the app, reset your progress on the “About” screen, or remove all data by uninstalling the app.

Purchases

The app is free and contains no in-app purchases.

Website and web version

This website and the web version of the app (/app/) are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the time and the requested page to GitHub's servers; GitHub needs this to deliver the page and to keep the service secure, and may store it in server logs for a short time (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in providing the website). GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

In the web version, everything you enter stays in your browser's local storage on your device, exactly as in the app. It is not sent to us or to GitHub. To make the web version work offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the app's files (service worker cache). We use no cookies, no tracking and no analytics on the website. You can delete all data at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

No tracking, advertising or analytics

The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no user accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
